ParkPal • Computer Exhibition Viva & Pitch Guide

Everything you need to impress the judges and visitors!

The Real-World Problem

  • Drivers spend an average of 15–25 minutes hunting for parking in busy urban zones.
  • Sudden encounter with "NO PARKING" signs leads to high towing fines and traffic congestion.
  • Excess driving burns unnecessary fuel and spikes carbon emissions.

The ParkPal Solution

  • NEXORA SCAN: Searches within 2 km of the selected map pin and returns up to five nearest approved stations with available slots.
  • Demo Availability: Sample open-slot counts, rates, vehicle types, and EV options make the exhibition flow interactive. They are not real-world data.
  • NEXORA Road Trip: Finds approved available parking within 2 km of the planned route, lets drivers select multiple stops, and builds one route through them. Parking is not reserved.
  • Community Registry: Saves user-submitted demo entries separately from generated scan results.
